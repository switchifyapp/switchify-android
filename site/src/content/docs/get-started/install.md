## What you need

- An Android phone or tablet on **Android 10 or later**.
- **A switch**, or a front camera for camera switches. See [Connect a switch](/get-started/connect-a-switch/).
- **Bluetooth Low Energy**, only if you want to control a PC. See [Pair a PC](/pc-control/pairing/).

Switchify does not run on iOS.

## Install

Install [Switchify from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enaboapps.switchify). Updates come through Google Play; when one has downloaded, the app shows a bar offering to install it.

## The setup steps

The first time Switchify opens it walks you through setup. You can go back a step at any time, and it remembers where you were if you leave.

1. **Welcome.** Choose *I'm new* or *I have an account* to sign in and restore your settings.
2. **Share diagnostic data.** Off unless you choose *Share diagnostic data*. See [Privacy](/privacy/).
3. **What best describes you.** User, specialist, carer or family, or other.
4. **Understanding scan modes.** Auto uses a single switch to scan and select. Manual uses several switches to move and select. You can change this later in Settings.
5. **Set up your switches.** Add an external keyboard key, a Bluetooth switch, or a camera switch. You can skip this for now.
6. **Enable the accessibility service.** See below.
7. **Pro.** An optional step describing Pro. Choose *Maybe later* to continue.
8. **Practice.** Four boxes to activate with your switch. Needs the accessibility service to be on.

## Turn on the accessibility service

Switchify works as an Android accessibility service, so Android has to allow it. Choose **Enable Accessibility Service** in the app, or open **Settings → Accessibility → Switchify** on the device, and turn it on. Android explains that the service can read what is on screen and perform actions for you; that is how it scans and taps.

Back in Switchify, the home screen says **Switchify is on**. If it says **Switchify is off**, tap it to go back to the system setting.

## Permissions

| Permission | When Switchify asks | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessibility service | During setup | To read the screen, scan it, and perform taps, swipes and other actions. |
| Camera | When you add a camera switch, or from **Enable camera** on the home screen | To recognise facial gestures. Only used while a camera switch is active. |
| Nearby devices (Android 12 and later) or Location (earlier versions) | When you set up PC control | To find and connect to computers over Bluetooth. On older Android versions, Location must also be turned on; Switchify does not use your location. |
| Internet | Only for the AI model download | See [Pro and your account](/pro-and-account/). |

## The free session limit

Without Pro, the accessibility service runs for one hour, warns you ten minutes before the end, and then stops. Turn it off and on again in Android's accessibility settings for a fresh hour. A new session cannot start while the device is locked. See [Pro and your account](/pro-and-account/).

## Your first scan

Close Switchify and press your switch on the home screen. The highlight starts moving. Press again when it reaches an app you use every day. If it moves too fast, slow it down under **Settings → Scanning → Scan Speeds**; scan speed is the setting most worth taking time over.
