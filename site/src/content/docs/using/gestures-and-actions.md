title: Gestures and actions
description: Taps, holds, drags, swipes, pinches and scrolling from the menu; finger mode; gesture lock and repeat; auto scroll; and recorded gesture patterns.

Everything you would do with a finger is in the **Gestures** submenu of [the menu](/using/the-menu/). The gesture happens at the point or item you selected.

## Taps

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tap** | A single tap. Also on the main menu. |
| **Double Tap** | Two quick taps. |
| **Tap and Hold** | Hold the tap for 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 seconds. |

## Drags and swipes

- **Drag** starts a drag at the selected point. Switchify asks you to select where to drag to, then shows **Confirm**, **Reselect** and **Cancel** so you can check the end point before it runs.
- **Hold and Drag** holds the item first, then drags it. Switchify asks where to move the held item.
- **Swipe Up**, **Down**, **Left** and **Right** swipe from the selected point.
- **Custom Swipe** lets you select the end point yourself, with the same confirm step.

## Pinches and scrolling

- **Pinch In** zooms out; **Pinch Out** zooms in.
- **Scroll Up**, **Down**, **Left** and **Right** scroll the content at the point.
- **Auto Scroll** (under **Settings → Gestures → Scrolling Settings**) repeats a scroll gesture on a timer until you press a switch, with a delay of 0.1 to 10 seconds between scrolls.

## Finger mode

**Finger Mode** in the Gestures submenu performs every gesture with 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 fingers, for apps that use multi-finger gestures.

## Gesture lock and repeat

These save reopening the menu for the same gesture again and again. Only one can be on at a time.

- **Gesture Lock**: the next gesture you choose repeats each time you press Select, without reopening the menu, until you turn the lock off or it times out.
- **Re-arm Gesture Lock**: turns gesture lock back on after each gesture, ready to choose another.
- **Gesture Repeat**: repeats the selected gesture on a timer until a switch is pressed. **First Repeat Delay** (0 to 10 seconds) and **Gesture Repeat Delay** (0.25 to 10 seconds) are under **Settings → Gestures → Advanced Gestures**.

All three can be toggled from the menu or from a switch set to the matching action.

## Gesture patterns

A gesture pattern is a recorded sequence of gestures that plays back with one selection. Patterns hold up to ten gestures.

1. In the **Gesture Patterns** submenu, choose **Start Recording**.
2. Perform the gestures you want, one after another, through the menu as usual.
3. Open the Gesture Patterns submenu again and choose **Save Recording**, or **Cancel Recording** to discard it.

Saved patterns appear in the same submenu. **Settings → Gestures → Gesture Patterns** is where you rename, reorder and delete them, and where two options live:

- **Manual Pattern Progression**: advance through the steps by pressing a switch instead of on a timer.
- **Stop Pattern on Switch Press**: any switch press stops the pattern that is running.

## Device actions

The **Device** submenu covers Recent Apps, Notifications, Open Assistant, Quick Settings, Lock Screen, Power Dialog, Take Screenshot and Volume Control. Home and Back are on the main menu. Any of Home, Back, Recent Apps, Quick Settings, Notifications and Lock Screen can also be given to a switch directly; see [Connect a switch](/get-started/connect-a-switch/).
